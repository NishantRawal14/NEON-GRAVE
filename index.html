05 — THE HOUSE RULESGOOD VIBES. SAFE NIGHTS.

READ THIS.
Respect that.

Come for the music. Stay for the madness. Keep the room good for everyone.

Entry & age ＋

18+ only. Bring a valid government photo ID. Tickets and security checks are required; re-entry is not guaranteed. Entry may be refused at the door.

Zero drugs. Zero violence. ＋

Illegal substances, weapons, fireworks and dangerous items are not allowed. Fighting, harassment, discrimination, unwanted contact or abusive behaviour means removal. Consent is always mandatory.

Drink responsibly ＋

Alcohol, if available, follows venue rules and local law. No underage service or disruptive intoxication. Plan a safe ride home — never drive under the influence.

Look after the space ＋

Respect the venue, staff and security. Damage may be charged where permitted. Keep restricted areas and emergency exits clear. Your belongings are your responsibility.

Photos & privacy ＋

Event photography/video may be used for promo. Tell organizers before the event if you do not wish to appear. Private or intrusive recording and sharing is not okay.

Tickets, changes & refunds ＋

Tickets are for this event and may not be resold, duplicated or transferred without approval. Personal cancellations/no-shows are non-refundable. If we cancel, refund information will be shared; changes will be announced through official channels.

Safety & organizer rights ＋

Report emergencies to staff immediately. Organizers may pause, modify, postpone or cancel for safety or unforeseen reasons, and may remove guests who breach rules, subject to applicable law.
